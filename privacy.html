Privacy Notice

Effective: August 2, 2026

Template notice for the self-hosted FrontSeat pilot. A qualified attorney should review it before broad commercial release.

Data storage

The self-hosted edition stores restaurant configuration, table status, waitlist entries, reservations, operational activity, and device settings in the browser storage of the device where FrontSeat is used. FrontSeat does not transmit that data to a FrontSeat-operated server in this edition.

Restaurant responsibility

The restaurant operating the software controls the information entered into it and is responsible for obtaining any required guest consent, protecting device access, maintaining backups, honoring deletion requests, and complying with applicable privacy and communications laws.

Guest information

Restaurants should collect only information needed to manage seating and reservations. Do not enter payment-card data, government identifiers, health records, or other highly sensitive information.

Backups

Exported backup and support files are controlled by the restaurant. Store them securely and delete them when they are no longer required.

Contact

The restaurant selling or operating its branded copy of FrontSeat should replace this section with its legal business name, postal address, and privacy contact email before distributing the product.
